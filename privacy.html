Privacy Policy

Chimera is a private, personal application used only by its owner, on his own computer, with his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Chimera reads and manages data in the owner's own Google account: Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Docs and Sheets.

What it does with that data

The data is used only on the owner's computer, for the owner's own use. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No Google account data is stored on any outside server by this application.

Other users

There are none. Chimera is not offered to the public and collects no data from anyone other than its owner.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
